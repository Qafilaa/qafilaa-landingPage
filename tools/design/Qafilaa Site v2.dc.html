U1 · Help centre

Answers, then a human.

Is there an app to track a group of bikers on a ride?

Yes. Qafilaa shows every rider in the group on one live map for the whole trip, not just a one-off location share. You can see who is rolling, who is stopped and who is resting, how far the convoy is stretched from lead to sweep, and each rider's last known position and how fresh it is.

Does Qafilaa work where there is no mobile signal?

Yes. Every screen reads from the phone's own storage first. You download the route corridor as an offline map before you leave, and navigation steps down gracefully: live route, then cached route, then stored geometry, then a straight-line bearing to the next rally point. Anything you do offline is queued and syncs when a signal returns.

Does the app detect motorcycle crashes automatically?

Yes. Crash detection reads the phone's motion sensors and is on by default. When a hard impact is detected a countdown starts so you can cancel if you are fine. If it completes, an alert goes to the riders closest to you first with your position, your bearing and your blood group, and it queues and fires the moment any rider in the convoy gets a signal.

Can I plan the Manali–Leh–Manali circuit with fuel and rest stops?

Yes. You build the trip day by day with legs, distances, altitudes and passes, mark rest days, and pin rally points where the group regroups if comms drop. Stays are scoped to nights rather than days, so one base kept for three nights is a single booking. Inner Line permits are tracked per rider.

How do riders split trip expenses?

Log any shared cost against the trip, choose who paid and how it splits, and Qafilaa keeps a running balance for every rider. Settle-up clears all debts in the fewest transfers. Amounts are held as integer paise so a split never loses money to rounding, and expenses logged offline reconcile later.

Does Qafilaa work outside India?

Yes. It is built for rides across India, Nepal and Bhutan. Trip money supports Indian Rupee, Nepalese Rupee, Bhutanese Ngultrum and US Dollar, with foreign spends auto-converting to your home currency at the day's rate.

What is a rally point?

A rally point is a place the group agrees on before setting off. If communications drop, everyone rides to the next rally point and waits there. It needs no phone signal to work, which is why it is the fallback the whole app is built around.

Does live tracking drain the battery?

Battery mode reduces how often your position is published on long straight legs and restores full frequency near passes and rally points, where knowing exactly where the group is matters most.

Frequently asked

Is there an app to track a group of bikers on a ride?

Yes. Qafilaa shows every rider in the group on one live map for the whole trip, not just a one-off location share. You can see who is rolling, who is stopped and who is resting, how far the convoy is stretched from lead to sweep, and each rider's last known position and how fresh it is.

Does SOS work without internet?

With no data, Qafilaa falls back to SMS — your emergency contacts get your last known pin, your blood group and allergies, and a link that opens once they are back in coverage. Tested down to 2G. The fallback is automatic. It still cannot reach anyone with no signal at all, and it does not call emergency services.

Does the app detect crashes automatically?

Yes, and it is on by default. Crash detection reads your phone's motion sensors on the device. When a hard impact is detected a countdown starts so you can cancel if you are fine. If it completes, an alert goes to the riders closest to you first with your position, your bearing and your blood group, and it queues and fires the moment any rider in the convoy gets a signal.

Does everyone in the group need the app?

The leader and anyone you want on the live map need it. Riders without the app can still be added as a name and phone number, and they will receive rally-point details over SMS.

How long is my live location shared?

Only while a ride is active, and only with riders in that convoy. It stops the moment the ride ends, you leave the group, or you flip Location off in Settings. Raw traces are deleted after 90 days, or 7 if you shorten the window.

What happens if my phone dies mid-ride?

Your marker goes grey and the group sees "last seen 14 min ago" with your final pin, so they know exactly where to backtrack to.

Who can see my medical card?

Nobody, until an SOS or a crash alert fires. Then the responding riders and your emergency contacts see a summary — blood group, allergies, conditions.

Can I plan the Manali–Leh–Manali circuit with fuel and rest stops?

Yes. You build the trip day by day with legs, distances, altitudes and passes, mark rest days, and pin rally points where the group regroups if comms drop. Stays are scoped to nights rather than days, so one base kept for three nights is a single booking. Inner Line permits are tracked per rider.

How do riders split trip expenses?

Log any shared cost against the trip, choose who paid and how it splits, and Qafilaa keeps a running balance for every rider. Settle-up clears all debts in the fewest transfers. Amounts are held as integer paise so a split never loses money to rounding, and expenses logged offline reconcile later.

Does live tracking drain the battery?

Battery mode reduces how often your position is published on long straight legs and restores full frequency near passes and rally points, where knowing exactly where the group is matters most.

How do I delete my data or my account?

From inside the app, or by email if you no longer have it. See Delete my data and Delete your account.

qafilaa.in/security
How we hold the things you trust us with

Security

Qafilaa holds a live map of where people are and, if they filled it in, their blood group. We take that seriously. No system is perfectly secure, so here is what we actually do rather than a claim that nothing can go wrong.

In plain English
  • Everything travels encrypted, and documents are stored encrypted at rest.
  • Accelerometer samples never leave your phone. Only the resulting alert does.
  • There is no public map, and no endpoint that returns a stranger's position.
  • Deleting your account removes the stored files too, not just the database rows.
  • Found a hole? Tell us at admin@qafilaa.in. We will not come after you for reporting it in good faith.

In transit and at rest

All traffic between the app and our servers runs over TLS. Position updates publish on a channel separate from application traffic, with a retained snapshot per rider so a late joiner receives the current state of the convoy on connect. Documents and images you upload — licence, RC, insurance, permits, trip photos — are stored encrypted, and the stored file is removed when you delete the record, not orphaned in a bucket.

What never leaves your phone

Crash detection reads the accelerometer continuously while you ride. Those samples are processed on the device and are never uploaded. What reaches us is the conclusion — an alert with your position, bearing and blood group — not the raw motion stream. Your phonebook is the same: if you grant contacts access, it is read locally so you can pick an emergency contact, and the address book is not uploaded.

Who can reach your position

Location is scoped to a ride, not to an account. Riders in your active ride can see you while that ride is active; nobody else can, and there is no public map and no endpoint that will return a stranger's position. When the ride ends, sharing stops. Access to production data inside Qafilaa is limited to the people who need it to run the service.

Working offline, safely

Every screen reads from the phone's own storage first, and writes are queued locally with a client-generated key so a retry after a dropped connection can never double-post. Cached data on your device is protected by the operating system's app sandbox and your device lock. If you lose the phone, delete your account and the sign-in sessions and position credentials go with it, so nothing can reconnect afterwards.

Retention, deletion and backups

Raw location traces delete automatically after 90 days, or 7 if you shorten the window. Server logs and routine backups age out within 30 days. Account deletion runs as a single transaction — it either all goes or none of it does — so an account is never left half-erased. The detail is in Delete your account and section 6 of the Privacy Policy.

Reporting a vulnerability

If you find something, email admin@qafilaa.in with enough detail to reproduce it. We will acknowledge within three working days and keep you posted while we fix it. Please do not test against other people's accounts or rides, do not exfiltrate data, and give us a reasonable window before disclosing publicly. We will not pursue legal action against anyone who reports in good faith and follows this.

If something goes wrong

In the event of a personal data breach we will notify the Data Protection Board of India and affected users as required under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and we will tell you what happened, what data was involved and what we are doing about it. Our Grievance Officer is admin@qafilaa.in.
